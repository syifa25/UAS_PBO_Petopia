📋 HOW TO PLAY 📋

🐾

1. SELECT YOUR PET

Go to MY PETS to choose from different pets with unique stats. Set your favorite as the MAIN PET.

⚔️

2. ENTER THE ARENA

Click BATTLE START! on the home screen. Choose your difficulty level from EASY to BOSS!

🎮

3. BATTLE MECHANICS

  • ATTACK: Deal damage based on your ATK.
  • POTION: Heal 50 HP (Max 3 times per battle).
  • SPECIAL: Unleash a powerful move (Requires 50⚡).
  • FLEE: Escape if the battle is too hard.
💰

4. UPGRADE & SHOP

Win battles to earn coins. Use coins to UPGRADE your pets' stats or buy new items in the MARKETPLACE!
